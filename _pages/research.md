Since February 2026, I have been a member of the [E-CoRe: Energy-efficient Computing via Reversibility research project](https://e-core.nws.cs.unibo.it/). This Doctoral Network, funded by the EU's Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions, is focused on applying reversible computation in several areas to increase energy efficiency. My work as part of this is focused on reversible distributed and concurrent algorithms.
